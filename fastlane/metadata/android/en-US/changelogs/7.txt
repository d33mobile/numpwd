* Remembered passwords are stored padded to a fixed size and bound to their key name. Passwords remembered with 1.5 still work; renaming a key now forgets its password.
* A temporary Keystore error no longer deletes a remembered password.
* Tapping two keys quickly no longer loses the second key's password.
* The clipboard is cleared after 30 s also when the screen was rotated or closed.
* The Copy buttons no longer stay greyed out when the code is ready very fast.
